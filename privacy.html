隱私政策Privacy Policy

原型草稿，正式上線前請法律審閱。 Draft – review before launch.

適用法規 Law

本網站依南非《個人資料保護法》（POPIA）處理個人資料。We process personal information under South Africa’s Protection of Personal Information Act (POPIA).

我們收集的資料 What we collect

僅限您在廣告合作表單主動提供的：姓名、商家名稱、Email、電話、訊息。Only what you submit in the advertising form: name, business, email, phone, message.

用途 Purpose

僅用於回覆您的廣告詢問，不做行銷名單，不出售。Only to reply to your enquiry. Not used for marketing lists, never sold.

同意 Consent

同意必須自願、具體、知情；「繼續瀏覽即視為同意」不成立。您可隨時撤回同意。Consent must be voluntary, specific and informed; continued browsing is not consent. You may withdraw at any time.

Cookie 與第三方 Cookies & third parties

本網站目前不使用 Cookie、不使用分析或廣告追蹤工具，字型使用您裝置內建的系統字型，不向 Google Fonts 等外部字型服務請求。We currently set no cookies and use no analytics or ad-tracking tools. Text uses your device's built-in fonts; no external font service (such as Google Fonts) is contacted.

地圖：只有在商家目錄頁按下「地圖」時，您的瀏覽器才會向 OpenStreetMap 基金會（tile.openstreetmap.org）下載地圖圖磚，對方因此會收到您的 IP 位址與瀏覽器資訊，依其隱私政策處理。不開地圖就不會連線。Map: only when you open the map on the Directory page does your browser download map tiles from the OpenStreetMap Foundation (tile.openstreetmap.org), which receives your IP address and browser details under its privacy policy. If you don't open the map, no connection is made.

本機儲存（localStorage）：語言偏好（hg_lang：繁、简或 EN）與原型點擊統計（hg_events，只記商家名稱與按鈕種類）只存在您的瀏覽器裡，不會傳給我們或任何人。清除瀏覽器網站資料即可刪除。Local storage: your language preference (hg_lang: Traditional, Simplified or English) and prototype click counts (hg_events, business name and button type only) stay in your browser and are not sent to us or anyone. Clear site data in your browser to delete them.

若日後加入分析或廣告工具，會先更新本頁並在需要時取得您的同意。If analytics or ads are added later, this page will be updated first and consent asked where required.

分享對象 Sharing

除上述地圖圖磚外，不分享給第三方，法律要求除外。目前預覽版放在 GitHub Pages，正式版預計用 Cloudflare Pages，主機商可能處理技術日誌（含 IP）。Apart from the map tiles above, no sharing with third parties except where law requires. This preview is hosted on GitHub Pages (Cloudflare Pages planned); the host may process technical logs, including IP addresses.

詢問結束後不超過 12 個月即刪除（待定）。Deleted within 12 months after the enquiry closes (TBD).

您的權利 Your rights

您可要求查閱、更正、刪除資料，或向南非資訊監管機關（Information Regulator）申訴。You may request access, correction or deletion, or complain to the Information Regulator.
